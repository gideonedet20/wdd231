Why by state

Parks aren't evenly spread

Nigeria's federal parks concentrate in a handful of states — Cross River alone holds two of the country's best-known reserves, while several states rely on a single well-kept urban park instead of a national one.

Sorting by state instead of by a flat national list makes it easier to plan around wherever a trip already starts, and to see at a glance which states pair a national park with a nearby urban option.
